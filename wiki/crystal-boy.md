# The Crystal Boy
![](banner_1)
A Game Boy you earn in game and play inside FFXI.

- Bluffnix's Gobbiebag quests give you the **Crystal Boy** and cartridges: recollections of past adventures found in crystals.
- Finish a game and hand it in to Bluffnix for rewards: a key item that gives **+1% experience** while Signet is active, a Page from the Dragon Chronicles, an Anniversary Ring and gil.
- It has controller support, fast forward and save states.
- Bring your own ROMs if you want the classic games. None are included.
