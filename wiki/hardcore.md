# Hardcore modes
![](hc_cards)
Press **HARDCORE** (beside DUALBOX) and pick a mode before your character's first level. Its rates are locked to that mode's profile wherever it goes: your world, co-op worlds and The Hub.

## Every hardcore mode
No auction house, no bazaars, no trades or deliveries with other characters. Squad gear only by trading it to your mates.

## Iron
![](hc_iron)
1x experience. Dying works like normal. Parties with anyone, and can step down to a regular character.

## Steel
![](hc_steel)
2x experience. No Home Point or Survival Guide teleports. Parties only with hardcore characters (your squad, no bot players). A Steel character that is knocked out sends **that job back to level 1**. Everything you completed stays, and squad mates keep their levels.

## Silver
![](hc_silver)
3.5x experience, the same limits as Steel. A Silver character that is knocked out is **gone**: it rests on the memorial and is deleted 7 days later.

## Randomized
![](hc_random)
You pick the name; race, looks, nation and your starting job are rolled. More jobs are rolled as you level, loot is randomized (but still fits), and you start with a random level 1 gear set and 10,000 gil. Not hardcore: trading and the auction house are allowed, and it parties with anyone.

## Milestones
Every 10 levels on a job, random cosmetics arrive in your delivery box: 1 for Iron, 2 for Steel, 3 for Silver. Milestones, Steel falls and Silver deaths are announced in your world, on The Hub and in Stinkshell (switch it off in the Hardcore menu). Each mode's shield shows beside your name.
