# Welcome to XI: Forever Offline
![](banner_1)
XI: Forever Offline (XIFO for short) is an offline, single-player take on classic-era FINAL FANTASY XI. The whole world runs on your own PC: a 75-era server based on Phoenix (a fork of LandSandBoat), a launcher, and Ashita add-ons.

## What it adds
- **Squad**: up to six persistent party members with their own jobs, levels, gear and roles. See [Squad mates](squad).
- **A living world** (optional): town crowds, adventuring parties and bot players. See [The living world](living-world).
- **Co-op and The Hub**: visit friends' worlds or the always-online official world, and bring everything home. See [Co-op & The Hub](coop-hub).
- **Hardcore modes**: Iron, Steel, Silver and Randomized. See [Hardcore modes](hardcore).
- **Tetra Master** and the **Crystal Boy**: card games and a Game Boy inside FFXI.
- A simulated auction house, a living conquest map, era-accurate experience, treasure caskets, fast travel and more.
