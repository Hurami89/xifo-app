# Squad mates
![](banner_4)
Your squad is up to six persistent party members who play alongside you. Each has their own job, level, gear and role, and is controlled by their own AI.

## How they work
- Mates are **level synced** to your character, so they always fit your fights.
- They get the **same experience** as any player in the party would, once per kill.
- **Gear**: trade a piece of gear to your mates once, and the whole squad can use it.
- **Gambits**: ordered rule sets decide what your mates do in a fight.
- Squad mates travel with you to co-op worlds and The Hub, and can heal your friends' mates there too.

## Hardcore characters
Hardcore characters have their own squads, managed from the Hardcore menu. Mates never lose levels, even in Steel mode.
