# Co-op & The Hub
![](hub)
Your world is yours, but you can still play together. Everything your character earns somewhere else comes home with it.

## The Hub
The official world of XI: Forever Offline, always online, with era rates and bot players. It is always at the top of the CO-OP page. Pick it and press VISIT.

## Friends' worlds
- **Host**: on the CO-OP page, HOST MY WORLD. Friends get an invite code (like KUPOMG-482-TRX).
- **Visit**: type the code under VISIT A FRIEND and press PLAY. Next time their world has its own card: one click.
- Works on the same network, over Tailscale or over the internet.

## Open worlds
Hosts can open their world to anyone. Open worlds are listed on the CO-OP page with their banner, description and how many people are there. Some have a password, and some take hardcore characters only.

## Good to know
- Your squad mates come with you and are real party members.
- Your place in an open world (linkshell, auction house, delivery box) is kept for 90 days after your last visit.
- A crash or disconnect is harmless: press PLAY THERE again. If a world has been out of reach for 24 hours, **TAKE BACK** brings your character home.
- Everyone in The Hub gets a white "Forever" linkpearl to chat with that world.
