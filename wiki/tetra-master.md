# Tetra Master
![](banner_4)
The PlayOnline card game, inside FFXI, with art by Nalfey.

- Start with **Luto Mewrilah** in Upper Jeuno: she hands out your starter deck.
- Card traders in each area's tavern sell starter cards.
- Collect 250 cards from card traders, monsters and treasure caskets. The good ones are rare.
- Play the computer for gil. Type **/tm** to open it.

## In this app
Open **Play**, then **Cards**. It plays with your character's own cards from the game, so it opens once Luto Mewrilah has given that character the starter deck (press PLAY in the launcher afterwards so the cards come over).
- **Against the computer:** Easy, Normal or Hard, the game's own rules and opponents. A win pays the same gil as in the game (a perfect game pays double), and a card that goes up a class keeps it. Both reach your character at your next PLAY.
- **Against another player:** Find an opponent and the hub pairs you with whoever else is looking. 45 seconds a turn. No cards change hands: it counts in your win / loss record.
