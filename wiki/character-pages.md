# Character pages & the Armory
![](banner_4)
Your characters can have their own page on xiforever.com: your face, race and nation, your jobs, everything you're wearing, your crafts and fishing, your fame and your squad.

## Put your characters on the site
1. In the launcher, open the **Characters** page.
2. Press **SYNC NOW**. Each of your characters gets a page at xiforever.com/c/Name.
3. Switch on **Update my character pages when I press STOP** to keep them up to date.

## Who can see it
Pick a character on the Characters page and choose:
- **Everyone**: in the Armory list and the Hall of Fame.
- **Only people with the link**: not listed; share the link yourself.
- **Nobody**: hidden.

Gil and what's in your bags are never shown on the site. The first player to sync a name keeps it.

## The Armory and the Hall of Fame
- **xiforever.com/armory**: browse and search everyone's characters.
- **xiforever.com/armory/fame**: the highest levels, the most jobs at 75, the best anglers, the longest adventures, the hardcore runs still standing, and the Silver memorial.

## On your phone
Press **PAIR APP** in the launcher and type the code in this app's **Me** tab. Your phone then shows your characters, hidden ones too, with your gil.
