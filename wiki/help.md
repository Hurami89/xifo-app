# Help & the Discord
![](banner_3)
## Found a bug?
Post it in the Discord's **#bug-reports** forum, one bug per post:
- What happened, and what you expected
- Steps to make it happen again
- Your version (Settings > Updates shows it)
- Your world (Quiet / Living / Living + AI)
- A screenshot or log

## Ideas
Post them in **#suggestions**, one idea per post. Say what you'd like and why it fits the era.

## Join the project
Testers, developers (Lua, C++, Python), artists and writers are all welcome. Pick your roles on the Discord and say hi.

## Support the devs
See #support-the-devs on the Discord, or catch the streams on twitch.tv/hurami.
