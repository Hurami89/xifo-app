# Portable fishing
![](banner_3)
Fish on your phone with one of your real characters. Everything you catch comes home to them.

## Get started
1. Pair this app with your launcher (Me tab) and press **SYNC NOW** in the launcher.
2. Open **Play**, then **Fishing**, and pick your character, then a region, a place and a spot. These are the game's real fishing spots, with the fish that really live there.
3. Pick a rod and bait, and **Cast**.

## The fight
When something bites, arrows appear. Tap the matching side (**◀** or **▶**) before the arrow goes away. Every right tap tires the fish out; a wrong or slow one gives it strength back. Wear it down before the time runs out. Gold arrows mean a critical bite: easier to land.

Watch the water while you fight: the fish's shadow runs the way the arrows point and comes closer as it tires, and a tired fish thrashes at the surface. Every fishing zone has its own pixel-art scene (the canals of Windurst, a mine in Gusgen, the deck of the ship to Mhaura...). Outdoors the sky follows the Vana'diel clock (shown in the corner, with the day of the week), so you'll fish at dawn, dusk and under the stars too. Each fish shows up as its own little sprite when you land it.

## Skill
Skill-ups follow the server's own rules, starting from your character's real fishing skill. Your guild rank still caps it: rank up at the Fishermen's Guild in the game to go further. The world's fishing skill-up rate (or your hardcore mode's) applies here too.

## Rods and bait
- **Rods**: the fishing rods your character owns. No rod? The guild lends you a Willow Fishing Rod.
- **Bait**: a free starter pack, the bait in your character's bags, or buy more in the **Tackle Shop** with your character's gil. Lures are never used up.

## Getting it home
Your catches are sent to xiforever.com as you fish (or with **Send now**). At your next **PLAY** the launcher takes them in before the game starts:
- the fish and items arrive in your **delivery box**,
- your fishing skill goes up,
- gil you spent on bait comes off, and gil you fished up is added,
- the bait you used from your bags is taken out.
