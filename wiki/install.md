# Getting started
![](banner_5)
## What you need
- Windows 10 or 11 (Linux and the Steam Deck: see [Linux & Steam Deck](linux)).
- A legitimately installed FINAL FANTASY XI. The project never includes Square Enix's game files.
- Update the game once through PlayOnline (a free trial account works). After that you play offline.

## Install
1. Download **XI-Forever-Offline.zip** (NEWS tab > GET XIFO, or the GitHub releases page).
2. Unzip it anywhere, for example `C:\XIForeverOffline`.
3. Run **XI Forever Offline.exe** and follow the setup. It finds your game, then downloads Ashita, the server, a small database and the add-ons you pick, all from their official sources.
4. Pick your world: Quiet world, Living world, or Living world + AI chat.
5. Press **PLAY**.

## Updates
The launcher asks you when a new version is out. Want test builds before everyone else? Turn on *Get test builds* in Settings > Updates.

## Stuck?
Ask in the Discord's help channels, or post in #bug-reports with your version, what happened and a screenshot or log.
