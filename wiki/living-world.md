# The living world
![](banner_2)
Pick **Living world** at setup and Vana'diel fills up.

- **Town crowds**: players standing around towns and outposts, chatting, selling and looking for parties.
- **Field parties**: groups of adventurers fighting at real camps (they won't take monsters right next to you).
- **Bot players**: real characters who level from level 1, craft, fish, run battlefields, do quests and missions, use the auction house and run their own linkshells. They keep progressing while you're away.
- They'll /tell and invite you if your job fills a gap. You can invite them, /tell them, and add your favourites to your friend list.
- **Bazaars**: what sellers shout in town is real. You can buy the item, or sell to them for gil.

## AI chat
With **Living world + AI chat**, bots talk through a local AI model (Ollama) on your own PC. Without it they still chat from a large hand-made dialogue pack.
