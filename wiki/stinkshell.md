# Stinkshell chat
![](banner_3)
Stinkshell is one online chat room for every XI: Forever Offline player, whatever world you play in. It's in the game, on xiforever.com and in this app.

## In the game
1. Switch **stinkshell** on at OPTIONS > Addons. It needs the internet and the launcher open.
2. **/stink** opens or closes the window.
3. **/sl text** says something without opening it.

Your screen name is your character's name. The first player to use a name keeps it, and anyone else gets a number after it.

## Who's who
- In game: the character's name.
- Website: **website: Name**
- This app: **mobile: Name**

## Rules
Be nice. Moderators can mute, kick and ban. In this app, long-press a name to block someone (only you stop seeing them). Trouble with someone? Tell a moderator on the Discord.
